## 2026-11-04: the Traces page, and long conversations you can read

- **Traces** in the dashboard lists every model call, conversation and managed session in one place, and rows on Analytics, deployment pages and the Agents pages open there. It replaces the Conversations tab under Agents. You can turn capture on or off for a live deployment from that page. See [The Traces page](/deployments/observability#the-traces-page).
- A conversation of up to 8 turns lists its turns as rows. At 9 or more, each turn is a bar on a latency map you can zoom, search and filter to failed turns, with jumps to the next failed and the slowest. A turn with more than 8 steps gets the same map for its steps.
- A conversation over 1,000 model calls now says so instead of silently leaving out the oldest: `GET /v1/deployments/{id}/threads/{thread_id}` returns `truncated`, and the dashboard shows a line. Managed sessions say so when they pass 5,000 events or 2,000 model calls.

## 2026-11-04: Ask AI: your answer on using your chats

[Ask AI](/agents/ask-ai), the assistant in the dashboard, has been open to every signed-in user since 2026-10-29, and now has its own page. Veri copies an Ask AI chat into a dataset only if the person who asked said yes, and no answer counts as no. `GET` and `PUT /v1/helper/consent` read and set your answer. Your own deployments are not affected.

## 2026-11-04: dashboard fixes

- The Deployments, Training and Datasets lists no longer stop at 50 or 100 rows. They read your newest 500 and say so when older ones exist.
- On Training, changing a filter or the search clears the rows you ticked. Before, a delete also removed ticked rows the filter had hidden.
- A stream dataset's rows read like an uploaded dataset's, and page 2 no longer repeats page 1. Streams don't have the jump-to-row box.
- Latency charts draw P50 and P99, matching their legend. The page size picker stays on Analytics and Traces when a page covers the whole window.
- **Set up your coding agent** on the empty Training page copies the setup line.
- App pages render at 80% on windows 1024 px and wider. The landing, sign-in and sign-up pages stay at 100%.

## 2026-11-04: corrections to the training docs

Nothing changed in the trainer. These pages described it wrongly, and a real training test on chat rows showed it:

- SFT's `max_seq_length` defaults to 1,024 (3,072 with `use_unsloth`), not the model default. A longer row loses its end, often the reply, and the job still reports `completed`. [Check row length](/training/sft#check-row-length) before you set or lower it.
- `messages` rows put a loss on every token, system prompt and tool definitions included. `prompt` + `completion` rows put it on the completion only. See [What the loss trains on](/training/sft#what-the-loss-trains-on).
- DPO's `max_length` also defaults to 1,024, and `max_prompt_length` has no effect.
- A `load_in_4bit` job reloads the base model in host RAM to merge, which can fail after training has finished. See [QLoRA merge and host RAM](/training/sft#qlora-merge-and-host-ram).
- A window import takes every captured trace on the deployment, whoever made the call. One conversation or one call imports over REST only: see [Import one conversation](/training/dataset-streams#import-one-conversation).

## 2026-11-03: low-balance alert, and an email when work stops at $0

Veri warns workspace owners by email when the balance runs low, once at $25 by default or every time it drops below a threshold you set on the billing page. When a balance runs out, Veri emails within minutes and lists the deployments, training jobs and agent sessions it stopped. See [Low-balance alert](/billing#low-balance-alert).

## 2026-11-02: turn trace capture on a live deployment, and one list for all traffic

- `PATCH /v1/deployments/{id}` takes `trace_bodies` and `trace_retention`, effective from the next call. Capture no longer has to be chosen at create. See [Turn trace capture on or off](/deployments/api#turn-trace-capture-on-or-off).
- `GET /v1/traces` lists every model call, thread and managed session of the workspace in one keyset-paged list, and `GET /v1/requests/{id}` returns one call with its body and a `body_state` that says why a body is missing. `GET /v1/traces/usage` shows the month's traces against your limits. See [Request traces](/deployments/observability#list-every-call-conversation-and-session).
- With capture on, a failed `/chat/completions` call now stores its request and whatever output exists: the error body, or the stream as far as it got. Failed calls are stored free for 14 days, up to 10,000 a month on Free and 25,000 on Pro: they don't count toward your allowance or your bill, and monitors and dataset imports skip them. See [Request traces](/deployments/observability#request-traces).

## 2026-10-30: the request log counts its matches

`GET /v1/deployments/{id}/requests` returns `total`, the number of requests matching your filters across every page, and `limit` now goes up to 500. See [the request log](/deployments/api#get-v1deploymentsidrequests). On the dashboard, Analytics charts start at the deployment's creation when it falls inside the picked window, and the recent requests table follows the chart window and shows how many of the window's requests it lists.

## 2026-10-27: the final model a completed job keeps is billed like its checkpoints

The final model a completed training job keeps in Veri storage (the final checkpoint, plus the merged weights of a LoRA run) is now billed monthly at the checkpoint rate, about $0.03 per GiB-month, prorated by the days kept, on its own line. It is kept until you delete the job, and the first month billed is November 2026. See [Model storage](/billing#model-storage).

## 2026-10-26: training job detail: live cost, lifecycle, setup events, step timing

- The job object gains `launched_at`, `provision_started_at` (billing start) and `terminated_at`, plus `user_rate_per_hour_usd` and `cost_so_far_usd` (what a live job has cost so far). See [Training jobs API](/training/api).
- `GET /v1/training_jobs/{id}/events` pages with an `after` event-id cursor and `order=asc|desc`; `limit` goes up to 500 and `has_more` is set when more events match. New worker events break setup down (`system_inventory`, `deps_installed`, `artifacts_downloaded`, `model_download_started` / `model_download_finished`, `model_loaded`, `first_step`) and time checkpoints and the final upload (`checkpoint_saved`, `final_upload_started` / `final_upload_finished`). The [event types](/training/api#event-types) are listed with their metadata.
- Every logged training step is stored, and the metrics gain per-step timing (`time/step_s`, `time/gen_s`, `time/reward_s`, `time/update_s`, ...) and GPU and host samples (`sys/gpu.{i}.util_pct`, memory, power, temperature, clocks, throttle reasons, ECC).
- New `GET /v1/training_jobs/{id}/files` lists the files a job was submitted with: reward sources, code archive, dependency file and entrypoint.
- SDK and CLI: `client.training_jobs.events()` and `.files()`, `veri jobs events <id>`, and `veri jobs get` prints the cost so far and the lifecycle. The MCP job tools carry the new fields.
## 2026-10-25: a resumed GRPO job keeps the source run's KL setting

- A GRPO job submitted without `kl_coef` no longer shows `kl_coef: 0.001` on its job object. The job trained without a KL penalty, but `client.training_jobs.resume()` and `veri jobs resume` sent the shown value back, so the resumed job trained with a KL penalty of 0.001 (and, for a full fine-tune, a second copy of the model in memory as the reference). The job object now shows `kl_coef` only when the job set it, and a resume carries exactly that.
- A job object shows `checkpoint_every` and `checkpoint_keep` as `null` when the job left them unset. Submitting that `null` back is now the same as omitting the field (it was a `400`).
- `kl_coef` must be a number of 0 or more: `null` or a negative value is a `400` at submit. Omit it for no KL penalty.
- `lora_dropout` may change on a resume; it is a training-time setting, not part of the checkpoint.

## 2026-10-26: a silent worker's failure says why

- A training job whose worker stops reporting for more than 15 minutes (900 s) still fails with `worker_failed`, but the message now says how long the worker was silent and the last status and step it reported ("Worker stopped reporting for 960 s (last status: training, step 40). ..."), instead of "Job failed". A job whose instance ended before its worker reported a result says that instead.
- [Custom scripts](/training/custom-script#keeping-the-job-alive): the worker keeps the job alive on its own (a status post at least once a minute), so a script that is quiet for a long time is not failed. Progress still comes only from `$VERI_METRICS_FILE` and mid-run checkpoints only from `$VERI_CHECKPOINT_DIR`.

## 2026-10-26: checkpoint storage is billed on the GiB you keep

A training job no longer carries a flat checkpoint-storage charge (8 GiB for a month, about $0.24, on every billed job). Its charge is GPU time only. The checkpoints your jobs keep are billed monthly instead, at about $0.03 per GiB-month, prorated by the days kept; a job that keeps no checkpoint adds nothing. Checkpoints are kept until you delete them, and the first month billed is November 2026. See [Checkpoint storage](/billing#checkpoint-storage).

## 2026-10-26: a job completes only with its whole model uploaded

- A training job whose final model did not fully reach Veri storage now fails with `upload_failed` instead of completing with files missing. `error.message` names each file and the reason. Files above 5 GiB upload in parts, so a large single weights file no longer fails.
- `GET /v1/training_jobs/{id}/model` and saving a job to your model library (`POST /v1/models`, `POST /v1/models/register`) return `409` when a completed job's checkpoint is missing a shard its `model.safetensors.index.json` names, or an adapter's `adapter_model.safetensors`. The message names the missing files.

## 2026-10-24: Explore and public run pages are retired

The dashboard no longer has an Explore tab, a Share dialog or public run pages. `POST /v1/training_jobs/{id}/publish` still sets a run's `visibility` and `GET /v1/public/runs` still lists published runs, but the `public_url` it returns does not open.

## 2026-10-22: LoRA-trained jobs deploy directly

- A job trained with `lora_rank` deploys like a full fine-tune: `veri deploy <job_id>`, `veri deployments create --model <job_id>`, or `source: "training_job"` in the API. Veri keeps the merged weights (adapter merged into the base model) next to the adapter and serves those. Pushing merged weights to Hugging Face is no longer required to serve a LoRA run.
- `veri deploy` accepts a completed training job id as well as a Hugging Face repo.
- Saving a LoRA job to your model library (`veri models save <job_id>`) saves the merged weights, so the saved model deploys too; `--artifact adapter` still saves the adapter alone for multi-adapter fleets.
- `GET /v1/training_jobs/{id}/model` gains `artifact_type` (`adapter` or `full`: what `files` holds) and, for LoRA jobs, `merged_files` (presigned URLs of the merged weights).
- LoRA jobs that finished before this change have no merged weights and keep the `400` at deploy; the message now names the Hugging Face route (`hf_push` with `artifact: "merged"`).

## 2026-10-22: a full provider quota is its own failure

- When Veri's own account limit on AWS is full (the vCPU limit that L4 and A10G boxes share), a training job or deployment fails with `QUOTA_EXCEEDED` instead of the raw AWS `VcpuLimitExceeded` text under `submission_error`. Nothing booted, nothing is billed, and `error.attribution` is `platform`.
- The message says so: "Veri's AWS capacity quota for L4-24GB x4 is full right now (a platform limit, not your account). Retry in a few minutes or use: ...", followed by shapes on other providers. DigitalOcean's GPU Droplet limit fails the same way.
- Capacity and quota messages name every cheapest shape that fits on a provider: a DigitalOcean H100 x1 failure now suggests both `aws` L4-24GB x4 and A10G-24GB x4, before DigitalOcean's own H200.

## 2026-10-15: GCP training on Flex-start VMs

- [GCP](/training/providers/gcp) trains on a single A100 80GB ($3.00 per GPU-hour) or H100 80GB ($5.99) on Flex-start VMs, in us-central1, us-east4, us-east5 and us-west4. Spot VMs are no longer used.
- A GCP job waits in `queued` for up to about 15 minutes while Veri looks for capacity zone by zone; the wait is not billed, and a job that finds none fails with `CAPACITY_UNAVAILABLE`.
- Veri runs one GCP GPU at a time for now: a GCP job submitted while it is in use waits in `queued` and launches when it is released.
- Two new failure codes: `provider_reclaimed` (GCP ended the VM early; credited back automatically) and `max_run_duration_reached` (the 24-hour limit, enforced by GCP). Resume either from a checkpoint with `resume_from`.
## 2026-10-15: model weights are kept until you delete them

- Checkpoints of finished training jobs no longer expire after 7 days; they are kept, with the job's final artifact, until you delete the job.
- Without `checkpoint_keep`, a job keeps every checkpoint it writes (the default used to be the newest 3). Set `checkpoint_keep` to keep only the newest N.
- Model versions have no default retention window (it was 90 days): versions are only purged under a window you set with `veri settings retention --days N` or `veri models retention <lineage> --days N`. The retention API returns `null` when no window is set.

## 2026-10-14: managed agents: every harness, triggers and session ops

- All six [harnesses](/agents/harnesses) are available on every workspace: `claude-code` (the default), `opencode`, `codex`, `langgraph`, `crewai` and `custom`.
- [Triggers](/agents/triggers): start sessions on a cron schedule or from a signed webhook (GitHub, GitLab or Standard Webhooks), with results to your webhook endpoints or a Slack channel.
- [Session ops](/agents/session-ops): run a command (`veri sessions exec`), copy files in and out (`veri sessions cp`) and open a shell (`veri sessions attach`) in a running session. Preview ports and GitHub access are coming soon.
- `ask` [approvals](/agents/sessions#approvals) park a Claude Code turn until you allow or deny the tool call.
- A workspace can have ten non-ended sessions at a time.

## 2026-10-09: volume-backed training, intermediate checkpoints, resume

- A dataset connected from a volume (`datasets.from_volume`) now trains: the file is downloaded to the training machine when the job starts, on every provider. The connect call checks the file exists (404 otherwise) and refuses paths with `..`.
- A job-level `volume` is a whole-volume read-only mount at `/mnt/<name>` (custom scripts also get `VERI_VOLUME_DIR`). To train on one file instead, connect it with `from_volume`.
- Volume bookkeeping: sizes are measured every hour (the `veri volumes list` size column and monthly storage billing are now real), a re-upload to an existing path replaces its size instead of adding to it, and volume names are unique per workspace.
- Intermediate checkpoints are on for every managed job: the default cadence is `checkpoint_every` 0.1 for LoRA and QLoRA jobs and 0.25 for full fine-tunes; uploads no longer race the trainer's own cleanup; periodic saves turn themselves off with a `checkpoint_disabled_disk` event when three checkpoints would not fit on the machine; every commit, skipped upload and disabled save is on the job's events. Checkpoints of finished jobs are kept for 7 days.
- `resume_from` on job create, `veri jobs resume`, and `training_jobs.resume()` continue a finished job from one of its checkpoints as a new job. See [Checkpoints and resume](/training/checkpoints).

## 2026-10-09: training jobs that cannot run are refused at submit

A training job that could not work is now refused with a `400` that says what to change, instead of being accepted and then failing on the GPU or silently ignoring a field:

- `checkpoint_destination` accepts only Veri storage (`{"type": "veri"}`, or omit it). Another type (`s3`, `gs`, `az`), a `uri` or `credentials` is refused, and the destination is no longer stored. Get the weights with `veri jobs download` (or `GET /v1/training_jobs/{id}/model`), or push them to Hugging Face with `hf_push`.
- `environments` is refused. A job-level `volume` is refused until the whole-volume mount flag is on; a dataset that reads from a volume trains (see above).
- `load_in_4bit` without `lora_rank`, a hyperparameter value of the wrong type (for example `"lora_rank": "8"`), an unknown `job_type`, and the `sft_video_gen` method are refused.

Multi-adapter deployments (beta): a request for an adapter that no replica has loaded yet (while it attaches, or right after a flip or rollback) now gets `503` with `Retry-After: 5` and code `upstream_unavailable`, instead of a `400` from a replica that does not have it.

## Multi-GPU training for SFT and DPO

`sft_text` and `dpo` jobs with `gpu_count` above 1 now run one process per GPU: data parallel when the model states fit one GPU, ZeRO-3 sharding (FSDP2) when they do not. New hyperparameters follow the Ultra-Scale Playbook: `zero_stage`, `micro_batch_size`, `gradient_accumulation_steps`, `global_batch_size`, `gradient_checkpointing`, `precision`, `cpu_offload`. See [Multi-GPU training](/training/multi-gpu).

Behaviour changes to know about:

- The default optimizer batch on a multi-GPU job is `micro_batch_size x gpu_count` (standard data parallelism). Set `global_batch_size` to pin it.
- `batch_size` on `sft_text` and `dpo` stays accepted as an alias of `micro_batch_size` and is now type-checked: `0`, `"4"` or `2.0` are refused at submit instead of failing on the GPU.
- `use_unsloth` and `load_in_4bit` with `gpu_count` above 1 are refused at submit (they were refused by the SDK only).
- New failure codes: `distributed_error`, `training_stalled`, `hardware_error`.
